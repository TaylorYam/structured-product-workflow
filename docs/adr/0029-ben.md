# 0029: BEN 沿用 FCN 的流程與欄位，新增參與率／BEN Reuses the FCN Workflow and Fields, Plus a Participation Rate

- 狀態／Status: Accepted
- 日期／Date: 2026-10-30
- 追蹤／Tracking: #372、#373、#374

## 繁體中文

### 背景

BEN（Bonus Enhanced Note）的發行上手以 HSBC、MS 為主，CA 少量，其他上手零星。每日匯入以前把它當成其他商品略過。

2026-06 以後的 TS／IIS 樣本機制相同：沒有配息、不會提前出場、沒有 KI，只在到期判定一次。最差標的 < 執行價就交股，≥ 執行價領現金。現金金額依樣態不同：
- 一般 BEN：面額 ×（100% + max(紅利, 參與率 × 漲幅)），樣本的參與率都是 100%。
- Digital：只領面額 ×（100% + 紅利），參與率是 0。
- Lookback（目前只有 MS）：執行價等回顧期結束才確定，另見 #374。

紅利是一次性的固定比例，不是年利率。交易明細的 Product 寫 `BEN`，下單信主旨分 Regular、Digital、with lookback。

舊總表的存法：
- Non-Call = 天期，比價日只填 `比價日_{天期}` = 最終比價日。
- `Coupon p.a. (%)` 填紅利。
- KO% 多半等於 K%，但有 K 90／KO 100 的寫法（說明書沒有 KO 價）。

起單建檔、每日交易清單都沒有參與率欄。

### 決策（2026-10-30 使用者確認）

- **商品種類一律存 `BEN`**，Digital、Lookback 不另立商品種類。在 `fcn_checker.products.PRODUCT_RULES` 加一筆：
  - `final_date_callable=False`：最終比價日不算 KO。
  - `maturity_only=True`：只在到期判定。Non-Call 要等於天期，KO Type 要是 `Period End`，Barrier Type 要是 `NONE`。
- **欄位比照 Super Bull（ADR 0028）**：`Coupon p.a. (%)` 放紅利、不年化；比價日只放最終比價日；到期 ≥ 執行價沿用「到期還本」事件，系統不算金額。
- **起單資料匯入自動套用**：BEN 不管起單建檔填什麼，一律存 Non-Call = 天期、`Period End`、`NONE`、KI 空白；好幾列在這幾欄不同也不用作業人員填。人工維護照同樣規則檢查。
- **KO% 照來源存**，比價、核對都不看。
- **新增參與率（%）**：
  - 存在 `workflow.product.participation_pct`，只有 BEN 有，其他商品空白。
  - BEN 起單匯入、人工維護新建、回補時預設 100；資料庫已有的值優先，所以作業人員改成 0（Digital）之後重新匯入不會回到 100。
  - BEN 空白、負數或其他商品有值都擋寫入。
  - 參考條件表多一個選填欄「參與率(%)」（`participation_pct`），資料庫來源帶出這欄，之後各上手的核對拿它和 TS 比。
  - 寄信草稿、商品總表不加這欄。
- **既有商品**由「匯入既有的 FCN 以外商品」（#303）回補：KO Type 一律 `Period End`（舊表的 KO(memo) 不看），參與率帶 100；KO%、Non-Call、比價日照舊表，Non-Call 不等於天期的不匯入。
- **寄信草稿、商品總表、風險等級**和 FCN 相同，主旨與 Product 帶出 `BEN`。
- **自動核對**：`[auto_check_products]` 登記 `BEN = []`，先不開放任何上手，照不支援樣態轉人工；之後依 HSBC → MS → CA 開放（#375～#377），只支援 2026-06 以後的範本。

### Lookback（#374，2026-10-30 使用者確認）

MS 的 lookback BEN 樣本：回顧觀察期間自交易日（含）起到 TS 寫明的回顧結束日（含）；各標的的回顧期初價格 = min(期初價格, 期間內共同預定交易日的最低收盤價)；回顧執行價 = 回顧期初價格 × K%；到期的最差表現 = 最終收盤價 ÷ 回顧期初價格。TS 在回顧期間結束前就發行，寫的是期初價格與期初執行價。

- **資料**：`workflow.product.lookback_end_date`（回顧結束日，可空白，只有 BEN 可以填，資料庫也擋）；`workflow.underlying_price.lookback_price`（回顧期初價格，只由比價寫入）。migration 0033。
- **輸入**：回顧結束日在人工維護、起單資料確認畫面填或改（起單建檔沒有這欄，資料庫已有的值優先）；要晚於交易日、早於最終比價日。MS TS 回填在 #376。
- **計算在比價**：比到回顧結束日以前在庫、原因「回顧期間（至 …）」；之後算出回顧期初價格（共同交易日 = 所有標的市場都開市；收盤價照拆股換到價格基準，ADR 0024；half-up 到小數第 4 位），執行價改成回顧期初價格 × 執行價(%)。期間內缺收盤價照現行「等股價」。
- **寫入與紀錄**：回顧期初價格與執行價和資料庫不同時更新，原值與新值記在 `workflow.backfill_log`（來源寫「比價：回顧期間至 …」）。核對條件重算時（還沒鎖住的商品、更正）保留同一位置同一標的的回顧期初價格，執行價照它算。
- **舊總表回補的商品**（已鎖住、來源「歷史回補」）：作業人員補回顧結束日後照樣計算；算出的執行價和資料庫的不同時比價待確認並寫出兩個值，不覆寫。
- **回顧結束日算過之後不能改**：已經寫入回顧期初價格的商品，人工維護、更正、起單資料匯入改或清空回顧結束日都擋。改了之後執行價要不要、怎麼回復還沒決定，先採保守做法，列在 [open-questions](../open-questions.md)。
- **畫面**：回顧期初價格寫在進度頁的比價原因（在庫時），人工維護打開商品時也顯示（只能看）；商品總表、寄信草稿、參考條件表格式不動（執行價欄在回顧後自然是新值；參考條件表的進場價維持 TS 期初價格）。

### 考慮過的替代方案

- **Digital、Lookback 各自一種商品種類**：比價和到期判定三種相同，差別只在系統不算的金額；交易明細也只寫 `BEN`。
- **參與率只放參考條件表、不存資料庫**：核對預設從資料庫產生參考條件表（ADR 0013），不存就永遠是空白，Digital 會一律被判不符。
- **KO% 強制等於 K%**：BEN 不會出場，KO% 不影響比價；強制反而會擋下舊總表回補。
- **起單資料照填的值存、不符就擋**：作業人員對 BEN 填哪些值還不確定，條件本身是固定的，自動套用比較不會擋單。

### 後果

- 每日匯入、起單資料匯入、人工維護、回補收 BEN；比價因 Non-Call = 天期不觀察 KO，最終比價日只判到期。
- `workflow.product` 多一欄 `participation_pct`（migration 0032）；參考條件表設定 v22 多一個選填欄。舊的 Excel 參考條件表與核對時沒有這欄的表照常讀，不算條件改了。
- 各上手的 BEN 開放前不能自動核對。
- Lookback（#374）：`workflow.product` 多一欄 `lookback_end_date`、`workflow.underlying_price` 多一欄 `lookback_price`（migration 0033）；比價可能改寫執行價並留紀錄；核對條件重算不會把回顧執行價算回期初執行價。

## English

### Context

BEN (Bonus Enhanced Note) is issued mainly by HSBC and MS, with a few from CA and occasional ones from other issuers. The daily import used to skip it as another product.

TS/IIS samples from June 2026 on share one mechanism: no coupon, no early exit, no KI, and a single decision at maturity. If the worst performer ends below strike, the note delivers shares; otherwise it pays cash. The cash amount depends on the variant:
- Regular BEN: notional × (100% + max(bonus, participation × gain)); every sample has 100% participation.
- Digital: notional × (100% + bonus) only, with a participation rate of 0.
- Lookback (MS only for now): the strike is fixed only after the lookback period ends (see #374).

The bonus is a one-off fixed percentage, not an annual rate. The trade sheet writes Product as `BEN`; order mail subjects say Regular, Digital, or with lookback.

The old master sheet stores BEN as follows:
- Non-Call equals the tenor, and only `比價日_{tenor}` is filled, with the final valuation date.
- `Coupon p.a. (%)` holds the bonus.
- KO% usually equals K%, but some rows have K 90 / KO 100 (the term sheet has no KO price).

Neither the order sheet nor the daily trade list has a participation column.

### Decision (confirmed by the user 2026-10-30)

- **Store every BEN as `BEN`**; Digital and Lookback are not separate product types. `fcn_checker.products.PRODUCT_RULES` gets one entry:
  - `final_date_callable=False`: the final valuation date never counts as KO.
  - `maturity_only=True`: the note is only decided at maturity. Non-Call must equal the tenor, KO Type must be `Period End`, and Barrier Type must be `NONE`.
- **Fields follow Super Bull (ADR 0028)**: `Coupon p.a. (%)` holds the bonus, not annualized; the only observation date is the final valuation date; ending at or above strike reuses the 到期還本 event, and amounts are not computed.
- **The order import applies the fixed terms**: whatever the order sheet says, BEN is stored with Non-Call equal to the tenor, `Period End`, `NONE`, and a blank KI; differing duplicate rows in those fields need no operator input. Manual maintenance checks the same rules.
- **KO% is stored as given** and ignored by observation and checking.
- **A participation rate (%) is added**:
  - It is stored in `workflow.product.participation_pct`; only BEN has one, and other products leave it blank.
  - The order import, new manual products, and the backfill default it to 100 for BEN. A value already in the database wins, so a 0 set for a Digital note survives a re-import.
  - A blank or negative value for BEN, or any value for another product, blocks the write.
  - The reference sheet gains an optional column 參與率(%) (`participation_pct`), filled by the database source, for the per-issuer checks to compare with the TS.
  - Mail drafts and the product master do not show it.
- **Existing products** are backfilled with 匯入既有的 FCN 以外商品 (#303): KO Type is always `Period End` (the old KO(memo) is ignored), the participation rate is 100, and KO%, Non-Call, and observation dates are kept; rows whose Non-Call differs from the tenor are not imported.
- **Mail drafts, the product master, and the risk level** work as for FCN, with `BEN` in the subject and the Product column.
- **Automatic checking**: `[auto_check_products]` registers `BEN = []`, so no issuer is opened and checks go to manual review as an unsupported variant. HSBC, MS, and CA open in that order later (#375–#377), for templates from June 2026 on.

### Lookback (#374, confirmed by the user 2026-10-30)

MS lookback BEN samples define the lookback observation period from the trade date (inclusive) to the lookback end date stated in the TS (inclusive). Each underlying's lookback initial price is min(initial price, lowest close on common scheduled trading days in the period); the lookback strike is that price × K%; worst performance at maturity is the final close ÷ the lookback initial price. The TS is issued before the period ends and shows the initial price and initial strike.

- **Data**: `workflow.product.lookback_end_date` (optional, BEN only, also enforced by the database) and `workflow.underlying_price.lookback_price` (written by observation only). Migration 0033.
- **Input**: the lookback end date is entered or changed in manual maintenance and on the order confirmation screen (the order sheet has no such column, so a value already in the database wins). It must be after the trade date and before the final valuation date. Backfilling it from MS term sheets is #376.
- **Computed by observation**: before the lookback end date the product is on book with reason 回顧期間（至 …）. Afterwards the lookback initial prices are computed (common trading days are days when every underlying's market is open; closes are converted for stock splits as in ADR 0024; rounded half-up to 4 decimals), and the strike becomes lookback initial price × Strike (%). A missing close in the period waits for prices as usual.
- **Writes and log**: lookback prices and strikes that differ from the database are updated, and old and new values go to `workflow.backfill_log` (source 比價：回顧期間至 …). Recalculating check terms (unlocked products, corrections) keeps the lookback price of the same underlying in the same position and derives the strike from it.
- **Products backfilled from the old master sheet** (locked, source 歷史回補): once an operator fills the lookback end date they are computed as usual; if the computed strike differs from the stored one, observation goes to 待確認 with both values and does not overwrite.
- **The lookback end date is fixed once used**: after lookback prices are written, changing or clearing the date is blocked in manual maintenance, corrections, and the order import. Whether and how to restore the strike after such a change is undecided, so the conservative choice applies until the user decides; it is listed in [open-questions](../open-questions.md).
- **Screens**: the lookback prices appear in the progress page's observation reason while the product is on book and, read-only, when the product is opened in manual maintenance; the product master, mail drafts, and reference-sheet format are unchanged (the strike column naturally shows the new value; the reference sheet keeps the TS initial price).

### Alternatives considered

- **Separate product types for Digital and Lookback**: observation and the maturity decision are identical; the difference is only in amounts the system does not compute, and the trade sheet writes just `BEN`.
- **Keep the participation rate in the reference sheet only**: checks generate the reference sheet from the database by default (ADR 0013), so the column would always be blank and every Digital note would mismatch.
- **Force KO% to equal K%**: BEN never exits, so KO% does not affect observation, and forcing it would block the old master-sheet backfill.
- **Store what the order sheet says and block mismatches**: it is not yet known what operators will enter for BEN, while the terms themselves are fixed; applying them avoids blocking orders.

### Consequences

- The daily import, the order import, manual maintenance, and the backfill accept BEN. Because Non-Call equals the tenor, observation skips KO and only decides maturity on the final valuation date.
- `workflow.product` gains `participation_pct` (migration 0032), and reference-sheet config v22 gains an optional column. Old Excel reference sheets, and sheets checked before the column existed, still read normally and do not count as changed terms.
- BEN is not checked automatically until each issuer is opened.
- Lookback (#374): `workflow.product` gains `lookback_end_date` and `workflow.underlying_price` gains `lookback_price` (migration 0033); observation may rewrite the strike and logs it; recalculating check terms does not revert the lookback strike to the initial one.

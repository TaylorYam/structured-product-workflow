# 0030: DACN 沿用 FCN 的流程，多存配息門檻／DACN Reuses the FCN Workflow with a Coupon Barrier

- 狀態／Status: Accepted
- 日期／Date: 2026-10-30
- 追蹤／Tracking: #366、#367

## 繁體中文

### 背景

DACN（每日計息的自動提前出場商品）是 FCN 以外量較大的商品之一，各大上手都有發行。每日匯入以前把它當成其他商品略過。

私人雲端倉庫 2026-06 以後的 TS／IIS 樣本顯示各家機制相同：每月配息 = 每期配息率 × 當期「所有標的收盤價都 ≥ 配息門檻」的天數 ÷ 當期一籃子預定交易日數，前幾期（通常第 1 期）保證全額，沒有配息記憶；KO 每日觀察、幾乎都是記憶式、從最後一個保證期期末開始，KO 價固定；到期依 Barrier Type 結算（NONE、EKI、AKI、少數每期觀察 KI），交股一律依執行價。樣本的配息門檻全部等於執行價，但各家名稱不同。

起單建檔、交易明細、舊總表的 Product 都寫 `DACN`；起單建檔沿用 FCN 同一組欄（`Guaranteed Periods (m)` 是保證期數），沒有配息門檻欄。

### 決策（2026-10-30 使用者確認）

- **商品種類存 `DACN`**，在 `fcn_checker.products.PRODUCT_RULES` 加一筆：Non-Call ≥ 1、最終比價日可 KO（和 FCN 相同），另訂 `has_coupon_barrier`。
- **新增配息門檻欄** `workflow.product.coupon_barrier_pct`（畫面欄名 `Coupon Barrier (%)`）：要配息門檻的商品（DACN）必填，其他商品要空白。起單建檔、舊總表沒有這欄，**起單資料匯入與回補預設帶執行價**；確認視窗與人工維護可以改，改執行價或商品種類時，配息門檻還是預設值（空白或等於舊執行價）就跟著換，填了別的值不動。人工維護和其他必填欄一樣可以先空著存，缺的時候核對條件停在待確認。
- **參考條件表新增選填的 `配息門檻(%)` 欄**（格式 v23，不在匯入格式裡）：從資料庫產生的表 DACN 寫配息門檻、其他商品寫空值寫法；核對時的表沒有這欄（v22 以前產生的）不算條件改了。各上手的 DACN 核對拿它和 TS 的配息門檻比，沒有這欄時比執行價（#368～#371）。
- **商品總表在 `Coupon p.a. (%)` 後面加 `配息門檻(%)`**（其他商品寫 `-`）；**寄信草稿不加**。
- **比價和 FCN 相同**：只記 KO／KI／到期事件日，**不追蹤每日計息**，不算計息天數、配息金額與贖回日。
- **風險等級**沿用 FCN 的公式。
- **自動核對**：`[auto_check_products]` 登記 `DACN = []`，先不開放任何上手，轉人工核對；依 Nomura → SG → DBS → Natixis 開放（#368～#371）。
- **既有商品**由「匯入既有的 FCN 以外商品」（#303）回補，配息門檻用表上的值，舊表沒有這欄或空白時帶執行價。

### 考慮過的替代方案

- **不另存，視為等於執行價**：不用動資料表，但萬一上手出現配息門檻和執行價不同的商品，資料庫無從記錄、核對也只能轉人工。使用者選擇另存。
- **匯入時空白、由 TS 核對回填**：核對前的寄信、總表看不到配息門檻，核對規則也少了可比對的值。
- **作業人員手填**：每檔多一個動作；樣本全部等於執行價，預設值幾乎不用改。

### 後果

- `workflow.product` 多一欄（資料表版本 0034），既有商品空白；必填與否依商品種類，由 `product_record.required_fields`／`missing_fields` 判斷，不放在資料表限制。
- 起單資料頁、人工維護多一個欄位；FCN 等其他商品填了會擋。
- 參考條件表 v23、商品總表多一欄；寄信草稿不變。
- 各上手開放前，DACN 的 TS 不能自動核對，比價日由回補或人工維護補上。

## English

### Context

DACN (a daily-accrual autocallable note) is one of the larger products besides FCN and is issued by most major issuers. The daily import used to skip it as another product.

TS/IIS samples from June 2026 on (private cloud repository) show one mechanism across issuers: the monthly coupon = the period's coupon rate × the days in the period on which every underlying closes at or above its coupon barrier ÷ the period's scheduled basket trading days; the first period or periods (usually period 1) pay in full, and there is no coupon memory. KO is observed daily, almost always with memory, from the end of the last guaranteed period, with a flat KO price. Maturity settles by Barrier Type (NONE, EKI, AKI, and a few period-end KI), and delivery is always at the strike. In every sample the coupon barrier equals the strike, but each issuer names it differently.

The order sheet, the trade sheet, and the old master sheet write Product as `DACN`; the order sheet reuses the FCN columns (`Guaranteed Periods (m)` is the number of guaranteed periods) and has no coupon barrier column.

### Decision (confirmed by the user 2026-10-30)

- **Store the product type as `DACN`** in `fcn_checker.products.PRODUCT_RULES`: Non-Call at least 1 and the final valuation date callable (as for FCN), plus `has_coupon_barrier`.
- **Add a coupon barrier column** `workflow.product.coupon_barrier_pct` (screen field `Coupon Barrier (%)`): required for products with a coupon barrier (DACN) and blank for others. The order sheet and the old master sheet lack it, so **the order import and the backfill default it to the strike**; the confirm view and manual maintenance can change it. When the strike or the product type changes while the coupon barrier still holds its default (blank or the old strike), it follows; a different typed value stays. Like other required fields, manual maintenance may save it blank, and the check terms then stay pending.
- **The reference sheet gains an optional `配息門檻(%)` column** (format v23, not in the import format): sheets generated from the database write the coupon barrier for DACN and the empty value for others, and a checked sheet without the column (generated by v22 or earlier) does not count as changed terms. Each issuer's DACN check compares it with the term sheet's coupon barrier, or with the strike when the column is missing (#368–#371).
- **The product master adds `配息門檻(%)` after `Coupon p.a. (%)`** (`-` for other products); **mail drafts do not**.
- **Observation works as for FCN**: it records only KO, KI, and maturity event dates and **does not track daily accrual**, accrual days, coupon amounts, or redemption dates.
- **The risk level** uses the FCN formula.
- **Automatic checking**: `[auto_check_products]` registers `DACN = []`, so no issuer is opened yet and checks go to manual review; Nomura, SG, DBS, and Natixis open in that order (#368–#371).
- **Existing products** are backfilled with "匯入既有的 FCN 以外商品" (#303), using the sheet's coupon barrier, or the strike when the old sheet lacks the column or leaves it blank.

### Alternatives considered

- **Do not store it; treat it as the strike**: no schema change, but a product whose coupon barrier differs from its strike could not be recorded, and the checker could only send it to manual review. The user chose to store it.
- **Leave it blank on import and backfill it from the TS check**: mail drafts and the product master would lack it before checking, and the checker would have no value to compare.
- **Have operators type it**: one more step per product, while every sample equals the strike.

### Consequences

- `workflow.product` gains a column (schema version 0034), blank for existing products; whether it is required depends on the product type and is decided by `product_record.required_fields` / `missing_fields`, not by a table constraint.
- The order tab and manual maintenance show one more field; filling it for FCN or other products blocks the save.
- The reference sheet moves to v23 and the product master gains a column; mail drafts are unchanged.
- Until an issuer is opened, DACN term sheets are not checked automatically, and observation dates come from the backfill or manual maintenance.
